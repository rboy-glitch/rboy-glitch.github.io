Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Spam Purger ("the app") handles Google user data. The app is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Gmail account.

Data the app accesses

The app requests full Gmail access (https://mail.google.com/). Google requires this permission for permanently deleting messages. The app uses it only to:

  • list the IDs of messages in the Gmail Spam folder,
  • permanently delete those messages, and
  • register for Gmail notifications about changes to the Spam folder.

The app doesn't read message contents, attachments, contacts or any other mail.

Storage

The app stores only its own authorization token, encrypted in the owner's Google Cloud Secret Manager. It doesn't store, copy or log email contents. Logs record only the number of messages deleted.

Sharing

The app doesn't sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and it doesn't use it for advertising or for training AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer contact email shown on this app's Google sign-in screen.
